Production possibilities

  • B INSIDE: IDLE RESOURCES, UNEMPLOYMENT
  • C OUTSIDE: UNATTAINABLE WITH TODAY'S RESOURCES

[ 03 // SUPPLY VS DEMAND ]

The trap: shift vs. move

  • PRICE CHANGES MOVE ALONG THE CURVE
  • INCOME, TASTES, EXPECTATIONS, RELATED GOODS SHIFT THE CURVE
  • PRICE UP AND QUANTITY UP? DEMAND DIDN'T BREAK. IT SHIFTED RIGHT.

[ 04 // NATIONAL OUTPUT ]

GDP = C + I + G + NX

C

Consumption

Household spending on goods and services

I

Investment

Business capital, housing, inventories

G

Government

Purchases of goods and services, not transfers

NX

Net exports

Exports minus imports (X - M)

[ 05 // CONCLUSION ]

Takeaways

  • 02 CURVES MOVE OR SHIFT. KNOW WHICH BEFORE YOU EXPLAIN.
  • 03 FOUR SPENDING BLOCKS ADD UP TO ONE GDP.
